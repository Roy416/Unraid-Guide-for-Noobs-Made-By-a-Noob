You may have heard about TRaSH guides and ARR setups. These allow you to automate downloading movies and shows. I can already hear you saying, "I'm perfectly fine doing it manually, I don't need to set anything else up". I thought the same way, and you're wrong. 

The three most popular tools are **radarr** (movies), **sonarr** (shows), prowlarr (source manager). A newer and cooler tool is going to come out this year and replace radarr and sonarr, so I'll wait to write section until then.
