# 📷 Immich

Immich is pretty much the go to "backup my photos from my phone to my server" service, it's the Plex of mobiles photos backup. Some features include; having multiple users, creating shareable links, having shared albums. It's also open source and completely free. It's still in beta, but it should be out of it Soon<sup>TM</sup>. They're working on it full time and updates come out regularly.&#x20;

Some oddities include, photos automatically being uploaded in the background doesn't work all the time which is not ideal to say the least, but they are currently trying out a new method. They also refuse to add built in 2FA, PLEASE ADD IT.
