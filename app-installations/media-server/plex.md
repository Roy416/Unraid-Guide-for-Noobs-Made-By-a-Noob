<summary><span data-gb-custom-inline data-tag="emoji" data-code="1f511">🔑</span> Key Term - Transcoding</summary>

* When you’re streaming a video on Plex for example and you need to **change the quality settings** (due to poor internet or device limitations), that process is called **transcoding**.
* **Intel CPUs can have** **hardware transcoding**, called **QuickSync**. This makes it way **faster** and more **efficient to transcode**. If you don’t have hardware transcoding and try to change the quality settings, it might cause you to buffer, or prevent additional users to stream.
* This is a paid feature for Plex but is free on Jellyfin.
